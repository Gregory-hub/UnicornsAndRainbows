# Ideas

## 1. Combo 7 heal

+1 HP on combo 7.

Combo is hitting an enemy without hitting the ground or getting hit.

7-section bar at the top with 7 rainbow colors.

Combo 7 must be hard to hit OR it must give you an item and you need to collect 4 of them to get hp.

Maybe +1 HP on combo 7 will make game too easy, so there are possible variations:
- give +1 HP
- give +1 HP each 4 of reached combo 7
- give some boost (one of which is +1 HP)
- give currency that you can spend at shop

## 2. Cloud fall-through

You slowly fall through the clouds when standing.

Falling on a cloud, you fall through part of it depending on speed.

If speed is enough to fall through, rainbow meteor-like rush burning aura effect is applied to the unicorn.

## 3. Seven levels, seven colors

The run is seven floors, one rainbow color each: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.

Each floor changes the mix (enemies, clouds, density).

After every floor except the last: perk or weapon choice. Then drop into the next color. No shop after violet.
